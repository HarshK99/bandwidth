These prompts are for changing `founder_operating_system_latest.md` separately. The original file has not been edited. They transfer the operating insights agreed during the Bandwidth discussion without turning the business document into an app specification.

Use the consolidated prompt for one edit. The focused prompts are alternatives for smaller edits, not additional passes required afterward.

## Consolidated prompt

```text
Update the attached founder operating system using the changes below. Preserve its strategic hierarchy, business targets, cash requirements, niche hypotheses, growth loop, evidence-first approach, and protected recovery. Do not invent new results, commitments, customers, dates, or current bottlenecks.

1. Refine section 9's energy philosophy into three flexible work modes:

- Deep work: thinking quality, judgment, problem solving, or important conversations materially affect the outcome. Guide: Think · Solve · Decide · Discuss.
- Production: sustained making or improving when the approach is reasonably clear. Guide: Build · Write · Edit · Refine.
- Light work: straightforward work requiring less concentration and easier to pause and resume. Guide: Research · Post · Follow up · Admin.

Use Production rather than Execution as the category name because sales and deep thinking also involve execution. Keep execution where it means doing work in the general operating loop or planning hierarchy; do not globally replace that word. Use Light work instead of Low-energy work as a block label. Keep Recovery separate and protected. The names describe the work's demands, not productivity or how tired I am.

2. Preserve the observed energy pattern. Use 14:00–15:30 as a starting fit for Light work and 15:30–17:00 as a starting fit for Production as energy improves. Peak/strong periods can support Deep work. These are working defaults adopted during the Bandwidth discussion, not established biological facts or a rigid timetable. Do not copy the preview's full day into a permanent weekly schedule.

3. Explain that actual task difficulty determines the mode. Building and editing may require deep work when the problem is difficult. Research, writing, and follow-ups can involve either judgment or routine handling. An activity may fit more than one mode. Do not assign all sales, research, writing, or development to one fixed category.

4. Keep business choice subordinate to the current bottleneck. Deep work or Production is not automatically a Wave Link or freelance block. Choose the business and activity from the current constraint. Either business, or both across available time, may deserve attention. Preserve larger blocks and reduced context switching; do not encourage repeatedly switching businesses inside a block.

5. Keep permanent block guides generic. Discovery and positioning can recur as evidence changes, but are examples of deep work rather than assignments for every deep-work block. Do not call them universally one-time activities.

6. Add these optional choices under personal life/recovery:
- Hobbies → Painting / Crossword / Language
- Family and friends → Call / Meet
- Life admin and finances → Pay bills and review finances / Run errands
These are menus, not mandatory habits, quotas, or new goals. Sleep, meals, breaks, exercise, downtime, and wind-down remain protected. Rest does not need a productive task attached. Do not delete existing hobbies merely because this short menu does not name them.

7. Retain the rule that tooling and learning earn time only when they remove a real bottleneck. Do not create a recurring tool-improvement goal.

8. If explaining Bandwidth, use a short paragraph: it shows the current block and a reusable activity menu filtered by effort. Its header displays two fixed reminders—Wave Link +5% weekly active-user growth and income ≥₹60k/month—with “Work: advance a goal or tackle its blocker.” It does not manage goals, measure results, or store strategy, bottlenecks, specific assignments, journals, or evidence. Those broader practices remain outside the app. Their absence from Bandwidth does not remove them from the operating system. Recovery remains protected; the reminder applies to work.

Edit only the necessary sections and keep terminology consistent. Preserve specific business strategy rather than replacing it with generic app labels. Do not remove practices merely because they do not appear in Coverage. Do not guess the dates behind “this week” or “next month”; flag those separately if needed.

Return the revised document and a concise change list. Distinguish the newly adopted work-mode framework from the original observed energy pattern.
```
